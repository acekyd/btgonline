# Exclusive: Inside the Minds of BTG's Top Teams

In this exclusive feature, we sit down with the captains and key players from some of Bravo Trashe Gaming's most promising teams to discuss their preparations, strategies, and goals for the upcoming tournament.

## Team Eclipse: Defending Their Crown

### Interview with Captain Alex "Storm" Rodriguez

**Q: How is the team preparing for the tournament?**
> "We've been focusing on our team coordination and communication. The new game modes require a different approach, so we've been practicing specific strategies for each one."

**Q: What's your biggest challenge this season?**
> "Maintaining our championship form while adapting to the new format. The competition is stronger than ever, and we can't afford to be complacent."

## Team Nova: The Rising Stars

### Interview with Captain Sarah "Nova" Chen

**Q: As a new team, what are your expectations?**
> "We're here to make our mark. We've been training intensively and studying the playstyles of established teams. Our goal is to reach the playoffs."

**Q: What makes your team unique?**
> "Our adaptability and innovative strategies. We're not bound by traditional playstyles, which gives us an edge in the new game modes."
